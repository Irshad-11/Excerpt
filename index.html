The flow

Two ways in, one archive.

Every capture — whether it's copied text or a shared screenshot — ends up in the same place, filed the way you chose it.

Clipboard Capture

Copy text in any app

Facebook, WhatsApp, a PDF reader — anywhere selection works.

Excerpt notices the copy

A quiet permission check, then a small "Save / Dismiss" prompt.

Pick a chat, right there

A floating picker appears over your current app. No switching.

Saved.

It shows up as a message in that chat, instantly.

Share Capture (Share Sheet)

Share text or image(s)

Multiple images in a single share action are supported.

Choose Excerpt from the share sheet

Standard Android sharing — nothing custom to learn.

Pick a chat, add a note if you like

Same overlay picker as text capture, plus an optional message.

Saved — and read.

On-device OCR pulls out the text so it's searchable later.

What's inside

A quiet archive, not another app to check.

Chat‑based organization

References live in chats, not note cards — captured items arrive on the left, your own notes on the right.

On‑device OCR

Screenshots and photos get their text pulled out automatically, so images become searchable too.

Language detection

Excerpt notices what language a reference is in, useful the moment your sources start mixing languages.

বাংলা translation

Extracted text can be translated into Bengali alongside the original — nothing gets replaced, only added.

Import & export

Your archive isn't locked in. Back it up, move it, or restore it whenever you need to.

Offline‑first

Everything is stored locally with SQLite. No account, no server, no waiting on a connection.
